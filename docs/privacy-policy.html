Who we are

Sakhi Samachar is a personal menstrual health diary made by [YOUR NAME], [CITY], India, for family members and friends. It is free and has no advertising. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What the diary records

Everything is optional and entered by you:

  • Each day: bleeding (none, menses, intermenstrual bleeding, unscheduled bleeding), flow volume (light, normal, heavy), heavy menstrual bleeding (HMB) if it affects your quality of life, counts of sanitary products used, number of clots passed, and a free-text note.
  • Settings: the sanitary products you use, your year of birth (used only for the FIGO age-specific regularity limit), and whether you take progestin ± estrogen.

Where your diary is stored

  • In your own Google Drive, in a folder called Sakhi Samachar (files Sakhi Samachar diary.json and Sakhi Samachar diary.csv).
  • A working copy is kept on your phone or browser so the diary works offline; it is merged with the Drive copy when you are online.
  • There is no Sakhi Samachar server or database. The developer cannot see, read or download your diary.

Google sign-in and permissions

You sign in with your Google account. The app asks Google for:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture — shown in the app so you know which account is signed in. They are not sent anywhere else.
  • Google Drive, limited to files this app creates (drive.file) — to save and read your diary. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Sakhi Samachar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the diary to you. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking, cookies for advertising, or third-party advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No prediction of fertile days or contraceptive advice.

Deleting your diary

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my diary and disconnect deletes the Sakhi Samachar folder from your Drive and the copy on this device.
  • You can also delete the Sakhi Samachar folder yourself in Google Drive (Drive keeps it in Bin for 30 days), and sign out on each device.
  • To remove the app's access to your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Sakhi Samachar → Remove access.
